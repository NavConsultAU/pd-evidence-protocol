## 0.2.1

Three proposals accepted (q4-run-note, approved by Ryan 2026-08-17), all three surfaced by running Q4 rather than by review of Q3. P-8: W-8c split. It was firing on 55 of 110 Q4 studies, mostly because the paper could not be reached rather than because it carries no funding statement, so it was penalising studies for our retrieval. W-8c now applies only to a confirmed absence in a text we actually read; new W-8d flags an unreachable statement at zero points and lifts on retrieval, following W-11's precedent. P-9: new governance rule G-7 requiring claims to be stated as positive assertions. Six Q4 claims arrived phrased as negatives, where a Priority 3 grade asserted the opposite of the evidence. P-10: design_base declared for meta-analyses of observational studies at 3, matching the value the control panel had been using undocumented since v0.1. Q3 re-graded: no claim moves. Q4 re-graded: see the v0.2.1 diff shipped with this version.
