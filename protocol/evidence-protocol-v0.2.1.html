0.1.0
Initial draft, 2026-08-11.

SR - source registry

What is allowed to count as evidence, and at what trust level.

S_authoritativemembers: cochrane_reviews, clinicaltrials_gov, anzctr, who_ictrp
role: Registries are the single source of truth for trial status and pre-declared outcomes. Cochrane outranks other syntheses.
A_peer_reviewedmembers: journals_indexed_in_pubmed_or_embase
role: Standard evidence. Journal-level check against predatory list (Beall successor lists) before admission.
B_preprintmembers: medrxiv, biorxiv
role: Admissible for horizon-scanning. Grade capped (see C-3). Flagged 'preprint - not peer reviewed' wherever shown.
C_greymembers: conference_abstracts, theses, registry_only_results
role: The excavation layer's raw material. Enters as leads; graded only if it survives extraction against the schema.
D_communitymembers: member_reports, patient_forums
role: Hypothesis generation ONLY. Never graded as clinical evidence. Feeds excavation (E-7) and the future member loop.
X_excludedmembers: press_releases, news_coverage, vendor_content, supplement_marketing
role: Never evidence. Usable only as pointers to a primary source. (The '70% flavonoid survival' figure died here in Q3.)

L - claim lanes

Every claim is assigned a lane at extraction. The lane decides which grading scale applies.

L-1_interventionquestion: does X help?
rule: Grades under design_base + modifiers + caps unchanged.
L-1_prevalence_safetyquestion: how common is X? is X hazardous?
rule: Observational designs are the appropriate designs here, so C-1 does not apply. SR/meta-analysis of adequate observational studies bases at 5; single observational studies at 3. W-8c records its flag against confidence but does not subtract (consistent with its own v0.1 note). All other modifiers apply.
L-1_risk_associationquestion: is X linked to getting PD?
rule: Grades under existing rules, always displayed with the 'about risk, not progression' wording.

Starting score by study design, before modifiers.

meta_analysis_of_rcts5
meta_analysis_observational3
rct4

case_control2
cross_sectional1

W - weights and modifiers

Applied to the base score. Each has an id so disagreement can be specific.

W-1_replicationvalue: 1
when: independently replicated by a second group
W-2a_small_nvalue: -1
when: n < 50
W-2b_large_nvalue: 1
when: n > 500 or pooled n > 1000
W-3a_double_blindvalue: 1
when: double-blind with credible placebo/sham
W-3b_open_labelvalue: -1
when: open-label or no blinding
W-3c_active_comparatornew_in: 0.2
rule: In trials where participant blinding is impossible by the nature of the intervention (exercise, diet, physiotherapy): W-3b does not apply if outcome assessors were blinded and the primary outcome is objective or rater-scored; W-3b applies in full if assessors were unblinded or the primary outcome is self-reported. W-3a and its bonus are never available to such trials. Replaces the control panel's unofficial 'strict blinding' toggle.
W-4_secondary_onlyvalue: -1
when: claim rests on a secondary outcome
also: cap at tier 2
W-5_surrogatevalue: 0
when: outcome is a surrogate (imaging, biomarker)
also: cap at tier 2
W-6_heterogeneityvalue: -1
when: meta-analysis with I2 > 50%
W-7_below_mcidvalue: -1
when: effect smaller than minimal clinically important difference
W-8a_interested_fundervalue: -2
when: funder sells or markets the intervention AND design is weak (open-label, surrogate, or secondary outcome). Strong design neutralises: a preregistered double-blind industry RCT keeps its score, disclosed.
W-8b_funder_in_conductvalue: -1
when: any funder (including nonprofits) participated in design, conduct, analysis, or manuscript - as disclosed. Triggered by Barichella 2016.
W-8c_no_disclosurevalue: -1
when: the full text was obtained and carries no funding or COI statement
note: counts against confidence, not against the finding; common in pre-2010 papers. Revised in 0.2.1 (P-8): absence may only be recorded from a document we actually read. Where the text could not be obtained, W-8d applies instead.
W-8d_disclosure_not_retrievablevalue: 0
new_in: 0.2.1
when: a funding or COI statement could not be obtained because the source itself was unreachable (paywall, captcha, robots block, dead link)
also: flag 'funding statement not retrieved'. No points. Resolves to W-8c or to a recorded funder the moment the text is obtained. Like W-11, this is a statement about our access, not about the study, and it must never cost a study points. [new in 0.2.1]
W-9a_single_centrevalue: -1
when: single centre and unreplicated
W-9b_geo_clustervalue: 0
when: positive results cluster in one country/region while multicentre trials elsewhere are negative
also: flag prominently (live example: FMT - positive single-centre Asian trials, negative multicentre European trials)
W-10_attritionvalue: -1
when: dropout > 20% without adequate handling
W-11_not_extractablevalue: -1
new_in: 0.2
when: key result cannot be fully extracted and verified from accessible sources (paywalled effect sizes, inaccessible supplements, figures without data)
also: cap at Priority 2; flag 'result not fully verifiable'. Cap and point lift the moment the numbers are obtained and verified - this is a statement about our verification, not the study.

S - stacking rules

Where two modifiers are symptoms of the same underlying limitation.

S-1When W-3b and W-9a both fire on the same study, apply -1 in total, not -2, and show both flags. They are symptoms of the same underlying thing: a modest single-site study without the resources for blinding or multicentre design. Each still applies at full value alone. W-2a remains independent - sample size is a distinct weakness.

X - external evidence

When general-population evidence may enter a Parkinson's question.

X-1_external_replicationrule: For a symptomatic, non-PD-specific outcome (the symptom and its treatment mechanism are not altered by PD in a way that would change the direction of effect), Cochrane-level general-population evidence for the same intervention and outcome counts as replication: W-1 applies, W-9a is neutralised, and C-4 is waived - provided at least one adequately designed PD trial confirms the direction of effect. Always displayed with the flag 'graded with external evidence', naming the external source. The PD-specificity judgement is recorded at extraction and is itself reviewable. Trigger case: macrogol (G4).
X-2_external_onlyrule: A claim resting entirely on external general-population evidence (no confirming PD trial) is gradable when the X-1 non-PD-specificity condition holds, but is capped at Priority 2 however strong the external evidence, and carries the flag 'no adequately powered PD trial - graded on external evidence'. If the condition does not hold, the claim stays Context. Trigger case: fibre (G8).

C - caps

A cap cannot be bought back by bonuses.

C-1max_tier: 2
for: any observational design, regardless of size
C-2max_tier: 2
for: pilot / feasibility trials
C-3max_tier: 2
for: preprints
C-4max_tier: 2
for: single unreplicated RCT, unless multicentre + preregistered + significant on primary outcome

T - tier mapping

priority_1label: Priority 1
gloss: strongest evidence
min_score: 5
plus: no cap in force; risk acceptable; effect noticeable
priority_2label: Priority 2
gloss: promising, unproven
min_score: 3
priority_3label: Priority 3
gloss: not supported by current evidence
rule: score < 3, OR pooled RCT evidence is negative for the claim. A negative meta-analysis outranks any positive single trial (this is what settles FMT and boxing).
pendingrule: definitive trial registered and unreported - never anticipate results
contextrule: background science and guidelines; not graded

B - claim-level grading

B-1Where a claim is supported by two or more independent RCTs and a concordant meta-analysis, the claim takes the grade of the pooled body of evidence, and W-2a is assessed against pooled n rather than against each trial alone. Individual rows keep their own scores; the claim-level grade is what the answer cites. Writes down what 'the grade attaches to the claim, not the study' already implies. Trigger case: progressive resistance training (E5, PRET-PD).

F - funding and conflict rules

F-1Extraction of funder, funder role, and declared COI is a mandatory schema field for every study. Revised in 0.2.1 (P-8): the status is recorded as one of three values, never as a blank and never as a bare 'not found'. found - a statement was obtained and is recorded. absent - the full text was obtained and carries no statement (W-8c). not_retrievable - the source could not be reached, so nothing can be said either way (W-8d). A study may only be marked 'absent' on the basis of a document that was actually read. Where extraction was blocked, the honest value is 'not_retrievable', and it is expected to change when retrieval improves.
F-2Funding is always displayed beside the finding. It never silently excludes a study - transparency, not censorship.
F-3Penalties only via W-8a/b/c. The trigger is the combination of interest and weak design, not industry funding per se.
F-4Any tier-1 claim in a published answer must carry its funding disclosure into the synthesis, not just the table.
F-5Non-financial bias is assessed alongside: researcher allegiance (a modality's inventor trialling it), geographic clusters (W-9b).

E - excavation layer

Where non-obvious findings live.

E-1Registry sweep: trials completed > 2 years ago with no linked publication. Unpublished nulls change pooled answers.
E-2Registry results database: outcome data posted to ClinicalTrials.gov that never reached a journal.
E-3Preprint monitor for the question's terms (medRxiv).
E-4Conference abstracts (MDS Congress and similar) - results surface here 1-2 years before journals.
E-5Contradiction mining: where community practice diverges from evidence (boxing), or evidence diverges by geography (FMT). Each divergence is a research question nobody has framed.
E-6Cross-study subgroup mining: signals consistent across studies but headline in none (sex differences in the diet cohorts).
E-7Community-reported interventions with no literature at all - the member loop as hypothesis generator. The one source competitors cannot copy.
ruleExcavation output enters as leads (source class B/C/D), passes the same extraction and grading as everything else. No shortcuts to the answer.

G - governance

What makes this controlled rather than vibes.

G-1Semantic versioning. Any rule change bumps the version.
G-2Every published answer is stamped with the protocol version that produced it.
G-3Rule changes are logged with rationale, and all affected claims are re-graded; the diff ships with the next answer.
G-4Clinician sign-off is a release gate for anything patient-facing. Reviewer: unappointed (open item).
G-5No ad-hoc overrides. A grade that feels wrong means a rule is wrong: propose the rule change, apply it everywhere, re-run.
G-7Claims are stated as positive assertions, phrased so they could be true or false, with the Priority reading as the strength of evidence FOR the claim as written. 'Drug X slows progression' graded Priority 3 says the evidence does not support it. 'No drug slows progression' graded Priority 3 says the opposite of what its own evidence shows. Introduced in 0.2.1 (P-9) after six Q4 claims arrived phrased as negatives and inverted their own meaning. Where a claim is restated to satisfy this rule, the restatement is recorded on the claim, and the change is a claim edit rather than a rule change.
G-6Every patient-facing artifact carries the standard disclaimer, verbatim: 'Priority ratings rank the strength of published evidence for each claim. They are not medical advice and are not instructions. Decisions about medication, diet or exercise belong with you and your treating team.' Wording changes to the disclaimer are rule changes (G-1/G-3). Final wording subject to clinician review (G-4).
